# Privacy Policy for Image Compressor Pro

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

**Image Compressor Pro** ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application on Google Play.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

### A. Photos and Media Files (100% On-Device Processing)
- **Local Compression:** When you select photos for compression, all image decoding, scaling, and re-encoding operations are performed **100% locally and offline on your device's processor**.
- **No Uploads:** Your photos, metadata, and files are **never uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party networks**.
- **Gallery Storage:** Compressed images are exported directly to your device's local storage (`Pictures/ImageCompressor`).

### B. Device and Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
- The app uses **Google AdMob** to display banner and interstitial advertisements.
- Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers such as:
  - Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  - Coarse device information (device model, OS version, language)
  - Non-personal app diagnostic and ad interaction metrics
- For more information on how Google uses this data, please refer to [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. Permissions Used

- **Photo Picker (`ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia`):** We use Android's modern photo picker to access only the images you specifically choose. We do **not** request broad storage access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).
- **Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used exclusively for loading Google AdMob advertising content.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Security

- Since we do not run cloud servers storing your personal data or images, we retain **zero** user personal records.
- All compression history and saved preferences remain on your local device and are wiped if you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android Settings.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is rated for general audiences and adheres to Google Play Families Policy requirements.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.
